About Me

My journey in tech started with a simple realization: to build truly great software , you first have to understand how it breaks. As a Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Student , I didn't want to just follow a traditional path; I chose to intentionally bridge two worlds that belong together — Full Stack .NET Development and Cybersecurity.

Day by day , I immerse myself in architecting scalable , high-performance web applications using C# , ASP.NET Core , and SQL Server , while sharpening my logic and data structures through competitive programming. But what sets my approach apart is running this in parallel with exploring penetration testing and ethical hacking.

By looking at code through both a developer's eyes and a security mindset , I don't just write functions — I engineer secure architectures from the ground up. For me , every line of code is a step toward turning complex technical challenges into robust , production-ready systems that are built to last.
